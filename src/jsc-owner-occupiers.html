Straight answers

Questions we get asked.

What does it cost?

Flat fee. Same number whether you buy at $600k or $1.2m, so we've got no reason to push you up. You get the figure on the call.

Why isn't the fee on the page?

Because the "free" mob will always beat a number. Free means a developer's paying them, and that's coming out of your purchase price. Twenty minutes on the phone and you'll see why.

Who pays you?

You. That's it. No developer, no selling agent, no referral fee from anyone we send you to. If we're ever shown to have taken a dollar from a developer on your purchase, we forfeit our success fee on it, and refund it if it's been paid.

What does the Full Property Disclosure Guarantee actually mean?

Before you sign, you get our written recommended price and everything we know about the house: flood and overland flow, easements, zoning, approvals next door, the comparable sales. The 65-point checklist, done, on paper. If we haven't given you that before you sign, you're under no obligation to proceed and you don't pay our success fee.

Should I wait for prices to fall further?

Nobody knows the bottom. The houses still falling are units, new builds and compromised stock. Good established houses in good streets have barely moved. Waiting is a bet the house you actually want gets cheaper while rent sits at $700 a week. We'll tell you what it's worth today and what we'd pay for it.

How do I know the agent's price guide is real?

You don't, and neither do we, so we don't use it. We price off recent comparable sales and what the bank will actually value it at, then we bid to our number. "Three other offers" is not a reason to go higher.

Will you tell me not to buy?

Yes. Nearly twenty times this year. If the numbers don't work at today's rates you'll hear it on the Kick Off Call, not after you've paid us.

What's actually guaranteed?

Three things, in writing. Full disclosure and our recommended price before you sign, or no success fee. Developer kickback, we forfeit our success fee on that property. Nothing under contract in 90 days, you don't pay our success fee.

What happens on the call?

Twenty minutes with one of us. Your monthly number at today's rates and whether a Strategy Session is worth your time. Nobody's selling you anything in twenty minutes.
